Orbita COTS Bioastronautics Architecture

NASA's COTS Philosophy: On the International Space Station, NASA pioneered the use of Commercial Off-The-Shelf (COTS) devices—like commercial iPads and ThinkPads—rather than spending millions developing single-purpose aerospace hardware. Orbita applies this exact philosophy to bioastronautics by pairing an accessible $40 Amazfit Bip U smartwatch with an autonomous local medical station.

Reverse-Engineered Huami BLE Protocol: Consumer smartwatches typically force telemetry into Chinese or third-party cloud servers. Orbita isolates the watch via macOS CoreBluetooth and Python (bleak), implementing the proprietary AES-128 challenge-response handshake on GATT 0x0009. This unlocks raw optical PPG streams (0x2A37), device event sniffing (0x0010), and bidirectional alert dispatch (0x2A46) completely offline.

Biometric Signal Processing: The station reconstructs Inter-Beat Intervals (RR) to derive HRV (SDNN) and calculates an Autonomic Stress Index (0–100) reflecting sympathetic fight-or-flight vs. parasympathetic rest tone. All data flows locally to the aerospace console at 127.0.0.1:8766.
