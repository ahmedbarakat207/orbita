# PRODUCT.md — AURA-OS BioFlight

## Product Truth
**Product Name:** AURA-OS (Astronaut Unified Resilience & Assessment System)  
**Event & Challenge:** 2026 NASA International Space Apps Challenge — *Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions*  
**Audience:** Astronaut crew members (Mission Commanders, Flight Engineers, Astrobiologists) on long-duration interplanetary exploration missions (e.g., Artemis Mars Transit, Deep Space Gateway).

## Operating Context & Mode
- **Mode: Operate (Aerospace Mission Critical)**
- Deep space exploration introduces communication delays up to 22+ minutes one-way with Earth. Ground mission control cannot conduct emergency real-time clinical consultations.
- The software must function 100% locally and offline as an autonomous clinical decision support system.
- Ergonomics require calm, high-contrast, zero-slop UI that minimizes cognitive fatigue during 24-hour shift rotations and emergency alarm events.

## Core Problem Space (NASA HRP R.I.D.G.E.)
1. **[R] Radiation:** Space ionizing radiation, acute Solar Particle Event (SPE) proton flux, cumulative lifetime exposure.
2. **[I] Isolation & Confinement:** Circadian disruption, sleep architecture fragmentation, cognitive vigilance degradation, behavioral fatigue.
3. **[D] Distance from Earth:** Clinical autonomy requirement, self-administered medical intervention, local countermeasure scheduling.
4. **[G] Altered Gravity:** Microgravity cephalad fluid shift, Spaceflight Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome (SANS), bone mineral density (BMD) loss, soleus/gastrocnemius atrophy.
5. **[E] Closed Environments:** Cabin ppCO2 elevation (hypercapnia headache prevention), trace toxic contaminants, acoustic noise exposure.

## Key Capabilities
- Real-time continuous biometric stream (Lead-II ECG sweep oscilloscope, plethysmograph, blood pressure, core temp, intracranial pressure proxy).
- Autonomous Edge-AI Diagnostic Triager detecting anomalies without Earth uplink.
- Actionable Countermeasure Hub with integrated mission timers (LBNP suction chamber, ARED axial heavy resistive workout, phototherapy, radiation storm haven protocol).
- Interactive Psychomotor Vigilance Task (PVT-B) for objective operational readiness certification.
- Crew Self-Reporting Clinical Journal with natural-language edge heuristic triage.
- Exportable NASA CMOS Medical Record for telemetry downlink to the Deep Space Network.
